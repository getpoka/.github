### What is Poka?

Poka is a personal finance tracker built around one principle: **your financial data belongs to you.**

We build tools that put users in control — no forced accounts, no cloud sync by default, no telemetry. Just clean, fast, and private finance management.

---

### Our Products

#### 🟢 [Poka CE](https://github.com/getpoka/poka-ce) — Community Edition *(Open Source)*
100% offline. Zero cloud. Your data lives exclusively on your device — a local SQLite database, nothing more.

- Multi-account & transaction tracking
- Budgets, goals, and debt management
- Split transactions & smart math input
- Built with Flutter · Drift · Riverpod · ForUI
- Licensed under **Apache 2.0**

#### 🔵 Poka Pro *(Coming Soon)*
Everything in CE, plus cloud sync, AI-powered insights, and multimodal features — for users who want more without sacrificing privacy.
